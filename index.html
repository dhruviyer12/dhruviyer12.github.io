Heartlander Electrical Architecture

CMU Robotics Institute · Surgical Mechatronics Lab · Jan 2026 to present

Heartlander is a minimally invasive crawler robot that walks on the surface of the beating heart. The lab has developed it for about 15 years, and when I joined there was no complete record of how it was wired.

  • Reverse-engineered 2 units and traced 40+ discrete components to produce the lab's first complete wiring schematic set and system documentation.
  • Redesigning the electrical architecture to replace point-to-point legacy hard-wiring, so sensor and external device payloads can be swapped without rewiring the robot.

Semiconductor-Domain LLMs

Panat Research Group · CMU Mechanical Engineering · Jan 2026 to present

Measuring how well Llama models fine-tuned on a 5-billion-token semiconductor manufacturing corpus actually adapt to the domain, and building reward models that score their reasoning.

  • Built ROUGE, METEOR and Jensen-Shannon divergence benchmarks for 1B and 8B Llama Instruct models fine-tuned on the corpus.
  • Chose GPT-4o as the JSD reference because it exposes top log-probabilities, and validated DeepSeek V3.2 as a lower-cost drop-in through an OpenAI-compatible client.
  • Developed the end-to-end pipeline for encoder-only process reward models that score tree-of-thought reasoning on manufacturing root-cause analysis.
  • Lead author on a paper about this work.
